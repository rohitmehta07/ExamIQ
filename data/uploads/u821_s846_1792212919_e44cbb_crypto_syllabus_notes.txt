Unit 1: Data Encryption Standard (DES)
DES uses a 16-round Feistel network with 56-bit key and S-Boxes to create diffusion and confusion.

Unit 2: Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)
AES replaces DES with a substitution-permutation network over Galois Field GF(2^8).

Unit 3: Classical Cryptography & Ciphers
Playfair cipher and Hill cipher use polygraphic substitution with matrix key multiplication mod 26.
