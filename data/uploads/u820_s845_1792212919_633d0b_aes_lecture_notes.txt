Unit 1: Advanced Encryption Standard (AES)

Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a symmetric block cipher standardized by NIST.
AES uses a state matrix with 10, 12, or 14 rounds depending on key size (128, 192, 256 bits).
Each round consists of SubBytes, ShiftRows, MixColumns, and AddRoundKey transformations.
MixColumns uses finite field arithmetic over Galois Field GF(2^8) with irreducible polynomial f(x)g(x) = 1 (mod m(x)).
